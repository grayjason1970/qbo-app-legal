Connect or Reconnect

Personal QuickBooks Bookkeeping Integration

This is a private integration with a single operator. There is no public sign-up and no customer-facing application. Authorisation is performed by the operator against his own QuickBooks Online company file.

To connect

  1. Sign in to QuickBooks Online as the company administrator.
  2. Start the OAuth 2.0 authorisation flow for the application, requesting the com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting scope.
  3. Approve the connection for the intended company file.
  4. The resulting refresh token is stored in the operator's encrypted secret storage.

To reconnect

Refresh tokens rotate and eventually expire. If the connection lapses, repeat the steps above to obtain a new token. No other action is required.

Scope of access

Only accounting data is accessed, and only for company files the operator owns or is lawfully authorised to administer. See the Privacy Policy for detail.

Privacy Policy

Personal QuickBooks Bookkeeping Integration
Last updated: 10 October 2026

1. Who this applies to

This application is a private integration built and operated by Jason Gray for the sole purpose of managing his own bookkeeping records in QuickBooks Online. It is not offered to, distributed to, or used by any other person or organisation. There are no third-party users, no customers, and no public sign-up.

2. What data is accessed

When authorised, the application accesses accounting data in the operator's own QuickBooks Online company file through Intuit's Accounting API, under the com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting scope. This may include:

  • Account registers, general ledger entries and transaction records
  • Vendor, customer and employee records
  • Financial reports such as profit and loss, balance sheet and trial balance

The application does not access payment card numbers, bank login credentials, or Intuit account passwords. It does not use the QuickBooks Payments API.

3. How the data is used

Data is used only to categorise transactions, reconcile accounts, produce expense summaries, and record transactions back into the operator's own QuickBooks company file. It is used for no other purpose.

4. Sharing and disclosure

Data is not sold, rented, published, or shared with third parties for marketing, advertising, profiling or any commercial purpose.

Accounting data may be transmitted to third-party AI service providers where the operator uses such a service to analyse or categorise his own records. Such processing is performed at the operator's direction and on his own data only. Data may also be disclosed where required by law.

5. Storage and retention

Credentials, including OAuth client secrets and refresh tokens, are stored in encrypted secret storage controlled by the operator. Any accounting data retained locally is held on hardware controlled by the operator and is deleted when no longer required.

6. Security

All communication with Intuit is over HTTPS. Access is restricted to the operator. OAuth tokens are rotated in accordance with Intuit's token lifecycle, and authorisation may be revoked at any time from within QuickBooks Online.

7. Your rights

As the sole user and data subject, the operator may revoke the application's access at any time by disconnecting it in QuickBooks Online, which immediately invalidates its tokens.

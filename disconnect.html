Disconnect

Personal QuickBooks Bookkeeping Integration

Access can be revoked at any time. Revocation is immediate and invalidates the application's tokens.

How to disconnect

  1. Sign in to QuickBooks Online.
  2. Go to Settings → Manage Apps (or Apps → My Apps).
  3. Locate this integration and choose Disconnect.

What happens next

The access and refresh tokens are invalidated immediately and the application can no longer reach the company file. Any locally retained accounting data is deleted by the operator when no longer required, as described in the Privacy Policy.

Transactions already recorded in QuickBooks Online are unaffected by disconnection and remain under the control of the company file's owner.
